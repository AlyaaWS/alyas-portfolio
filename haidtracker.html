Work Project

Haidtracker

Year 2024
Role Full Stack Developer
Client Transportation Dept. of Sleman

Project Description

The local government faced challenges in tracking and maintaining street lighting across the region. Reports from citizens were often lost, and field technicians lacked a centralized system to update maintenance statuses.

Haidtracker is a web-based reporting system that utilizes barcode scanning. It allows citizens to easily report broken streetlights by scanning a barcode on the pole, and provides administrators and technicians with a comprehensive dashboard to track, assign, and update repair statuses in real-time.

My Contribution

As the Full Stack Developer, I designed and developed the entire application from the ground up using Laravel and MySQL. I implemented the barcode generation for assets, the public-facing reporting interface, and the secure administrative dashboard for internal use.

Impact

The system significantly streamlined the maintenance workflow for the department, creating a transparent and trackable pipeline from a citizen's report to the final repair.
