SIAP MAJU

Integrated Street Lighting Asset Management System

Year 2026
Role Freelance Full Stack Web Developer
Client Dinas Perhubungan Sleman

Project Description

SIAP MAJU is a web-based street lighting asset management and reporting system designed to support administrative and field operations. The administrative dashboard provides a centralized platform for managing street lighting assets, monitoring public reports, and supporting maintenance activities.

My Contribution

As the Full Stack Web Developer, I developed the administrative dashboard using PHP and MySQL. I worked on the street lighting asset management, report monitoring, barcode-based asset identification, and centralized data workflows to support administrators and field personnel in managing operational information.

Technical Approach

The system was developed using PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and Chart.js. The administrative dashboard works with centralized asset records, allowing street lighting assets identified by asset IDs and barcodes to be managed alongside their related reporting information.

Impact

The system centralized the management and monitoring of 700+ street lighting assets, replacing spreadsheet-based processes with a centralized digital system. It also supported field operations through system demonstrations and training for approximately 10 field personnel.
