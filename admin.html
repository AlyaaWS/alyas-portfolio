Work Assignment

SIAP MAJU

Integrated Street Lighting Asset Management System

Year 2026
Role Freelance Full Stack Web Developer
Client Dinas Perhubungan Sleman

Project Description

SIAP MAJU is a street lighting asset management system designed to centralize asset records, public reports, and maintenance monitoring in one administrative platform.

My Contribution

As the Full-Stack Web Developer, I developed the administrative dashboard using PHP and MySQL. My work included asset management, public report management, QR code and asset ID-based identification, and monitoring features to support administrative workflows and field operations.

Technical Approach

Built using PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and Chart.js, the dashboard connects street lighting asset records with their locations, identification codes, and related reports. Data visualization and centralized records help administrators monitor assets and access operational information in one place.

Impact

Centralized the management and monitoring of 700+ street lighting assets, replacing spreadsheet-based processes with a digital system. Also supported system adoption through demonstrations and training for approximately 10 field personnel.
