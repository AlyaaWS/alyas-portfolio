Work Assignment

SIAP MAJU

Integrated Street Lighting Asset Management System

Year 2026
Role Freelance Full Stack Web Developer
Client Dinas Perhubungan Sleman

Project Description

SIAP MAJU is a web-based street lighting asset management and reporting system designed to connect public reporting with administrative and field operations. The system links physical street lighting assets to digital records through asset IDs and barcodes, while providing a centralized platform for monitoring and reporting.

My Contribution

As the Full Stack Web Developer, I developed the connected public reporting interface and administrative dashboard using PHP and MySQL. I worked on the asset management, reporting, barcode-based asset identification, and centralized monitoring workflows, and supported system adoption through user demonstrations and training.

Impact

The system centralized monitoring and reporting for 700+ street lighting assets, replacing spreadsheet-based processes with a centralized digital system. It also supported field operations through system demonstrations and training for approximately 10 field personnel.

What I Learned

This project taught me how to design connected workflows for different types of users, from public reporting to administrative and field operations. I also learned how to connect physical assets with digital records through asset IDs and barcodes while keeping the experience practical for users in the field.
